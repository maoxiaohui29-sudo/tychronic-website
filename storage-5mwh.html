5 MWh BESS questions, answered

What is a 5 MWh BESS container?

A centralized battery storage system in a 20 ft container. StarCharge's unit holds 5,015.96 kWh LFP in a 1P104S / 1P416S, 12-cluster arrangement.

What are the published specifications?

5,015.96 kWh, 1331.2 V nominal, 0.5P, 94% or higher RTE, IP55, -30 to 55 deg C, about 43 t.

How much does a 5 MWh BESS cost?

Depends on quantity, grid interface, delivery and site works. Itemised quotes are issued on request.

Which certifications are listed?

IEC 62619, UL 9540A, UL 9540 and NFPA 855.

What is the round-trip efficiency?

94% or higher, with smart liquid cooling.

What temperature range is supported?

-30 to 55 deg C, IP55.

How is the system configured internally?

1P104S / 1P416S across 12 clusters at 1331.2 V nominal.

What charge rate does it support?

0.5P charge and discharge.

Are there completed projects?

Yes: 10 MW / 20 MWh Plovdiv, commissioning planned April 2026, and 25 MW / 50 MWh Latvia, delivery Q2 2026.

Is it suitable for European utility projects?

Yes. The Latvia design was approved by local authorities and is StarCharge's first European utility-scale storage contract in 2026.

5 MWh or 261 kWh — which fits?

261 kWh suits commercial and industrial sites; 5 MWh suits utility and grid-side projects. Send capacity and grid requirements for a shortlist.

Can Tychronic supply 5 MWh containers to EPCs?

Yes. We source StarCharge equipment for overseas EPCs, developers and utilities and coordinate delivery.
