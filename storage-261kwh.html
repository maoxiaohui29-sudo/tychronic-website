261 kWh cabinet questions, answered

What is a 261 kWh cabinet used for?

Peak shaving, demand-charge reduction, solar self-consumption and backup capacity for EV charging hubs.

Which verified 261 kWh models are listed?

XES-M125/261-YB, 125 kW / 261 kWh all-in-one, and ETANK-5S, 261 kWh liquid-cooled cabinet. StarCharge's eBox-261 datasheet is pending.

What cells are used?

LFP 3.2 V / 314 Ah cells in both verified XES models.

What is the AC output and PV input?

The all-in-one delivers 125 kW AC and accepts 250 kW PV input up to 1000 V via 10 MPPT.

What is the voltage and charge rate of ETANK-5S?

832 V nominal, 650-949 V range, 0.5C charge and discharge.

How efficient is the system?

ETANK-5S is specified at 90% or higher system efficiency.

What cycle life is specified?

ETANK-5S: 8000 cycles to 70% end of life. All-in-one cycle life is not stated in the source.

What are the site conditions?

All-in-one: IP54, -25 to 55 deg C, up to 2000 m. ETANK-5S: IP54, -20 to 55 deg C.

How big and heavy is the all-in-one?

1485 x 1422 x 2343 mm, approximately 2810 kg.

Can it work with solar PV?

Yes — the all-in-one accepts 250 kW PV at up to 1000 V, 10 MPPT. ETANK-5S is storage only.

Is it suitable for an EV charging site?

Yes, where demand charges or grid capacity are the constraint. Send charger rating and connection capacity for a shortlist.

How much does it cost?

Depends on model, volume, shipping and site scope. Itemised quotes are provided on request.

Can Tychronic supply from China to overseas projects?

Yes. Tychronic sources, checks specifications and coordinates delivery for overseas buyers.

What fire protection is fitted?

ETANK-5S has triple fire protection. All-in-one details are not in the supplied source.

Which interfaces are supported?

ETANK-5S: RS485, CAN and LAN; noise 70 dB or less.
